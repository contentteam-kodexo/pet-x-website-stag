Benefits of Groom-X

The First Check-In Assistant Tool for Pet Grooming

We’re proud to introduce the world’s first Check-In Assistant application designed specifically for the pet industry. Our flagship product, Groom-X, empowers pet professionals by improving communication with pet owners through realistic images of grooming styles, helping clients visualize outcomes, set clear expectations, and enhance satisfaction.

  1. Groom-X uses an advanced software technology to show how a pet will look after grooming by analyzing its breed, coat type and following groomers’ instructions. This helps choose the best style with accuracy and avoid disappointing surprises.
  2. Groom-X makes it easy to duplicate sessions when your recurring customers love the style they got previously. This makes the check-in process easier after a thorough pet assessment of skin and coat.
  3. Groom-X tracks the tools used for each grooming session and predicts when they need to be maintained or replaced to avoid pet injuries and unexpected breakdowns.
  4. Groom-X allows business owners and managers to have a quick overview of their business trends in a easy to read dashboard, providing insights and year-over-year comparisons.
